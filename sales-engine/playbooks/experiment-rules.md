# Commercial Experiment Rules

Every new sales motion is a bounded experiment.

## Required before launch

- Audience
- Offer
- Channel
- Proof asset/demo
- CTA
- Contact volume
- Test window
- Success gate
- Stop/rewrite rule
- Owner

## Interpretation

- **No substantive replies after threshold** → change/stop hypothesis.
- **Replies but no demos** → inspect CTA, qualification and decision-maker access.
- **Demos but no paid entry** → inspect value, trust, price and entry scope.
- **Paid entry but no expansion** → inspect delivery result and cross-sell trigger.
- **Won/Lost** → feed evidence back into Products, Offers and Experiments.

## Experimental discipline

Do not change audience, offer and channel at the same time. At least one dimension must remain stable so results can be interpreted.

## Default first-contact rule

One observed problem + one concrete result + one CTA.

Do not send a large personalized proposal by default before interest.

## Long-cycle rule

D/E deals stay active in parallel with S/A cashflow experiments. They are not penalized solely for a longer cycle; ticket potential is evaluated separately.
