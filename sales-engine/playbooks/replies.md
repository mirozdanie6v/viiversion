# VIIVERSION Reply Playbook v1.1

## Mandatory client-context preflight
Before classifying or answering any client reply, resolve the named company/person to the existing lead record and load **Sales_Router + Outreach_Queue + available communication history**. Determine current stage, latest client message/reaction, what VIIVERSION already sent, current next action and known open questions.

Do not draft from the isolated last message. Do not restart a cold introduction for a relationship already at Replied/Demo/Discovery/Proposal/Negotiation. If the user mentions a known client in ordinary language, this preflight applies even without an explicit `ПРОДАЖИ` command.

When interpreting a short reply, emoji/reaction or silence, separate **FACT / INFERENCE / CONFIDENCE**. Never convert inference into recorded fact.

## Interested / "send demo"
Send the single most relevant asset. Then ask one diagnostic question tied to the workflow. Do not add a second pitch.

## "How much?"
If the offer has a supported price/range, answer directly and explain what is included. If price depends on scope/integration, state the dependency and offer the smallest scoping step. Do not hide the number merely to force a call.

## "We already have a system"
Acknowledge it. Ask one edge-case question: where does the current system still require chat, manual transfer, duplicate entry, or an external channel? If no meaningful gap appears, stop.

## "Send information"
Send one asset with a one-line explanation of what to look at. Do not send a long proposal unless requested.

## Not interested
Acknowledge, thank them, mark reason if they volunteered one, stop the sequence. Do not argue.

## Wrong person
Ask: "Who is responsible for [specific workflow]?" Keep the referral request short.

## "Who are you?"
Answer in one line: VIIVERSION is a product/engineering team building booking, customer-flow, integration and automation modules. Then immediately reconnect to the relevant proof.

## Positive after demo
Ask one fit question:
- "How is this step handled today?"
- "Would the team need approval before a request is confirmed?"
- "Which channel creates the most manual back-and-forth?"

## Qualified
Only now move to a concrete meeting, discovery, pilot, proposal or paid entry step.
