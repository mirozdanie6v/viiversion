# VIIVERSION Outreach Playbook v1.1

## Operating principle
Copy is not judged by taste. It is produced from account evidence, buyer role, offer fit, proof and experiment rules. Pre-send quality is separated from market performance.

## Who owns what
- Product marketing: ICP, problem, positioning, offer and reason-to-buy.
- Conversion copywriting: value proposition, hierarchy, clarity and CTA.
- Direct response: one-message/one-action discipline and proof.
- SDR/outbound: account signals, person/channel, cadence and follow-up.
- Sales enablement: proof kit, persona maps, reply handling and templates.
- CRO: controlled variants and conversion measurement.

## Pre-send model
Before a message becomes Ready, score:
1. Signal strength 0-2.
2. Product/market fit 0-2.
3. Message clarity 0-1.
4. Proof strength 0-2.
5. CTA friction 0-1.
6. Localization 0-1.
7. Claim safety 0-1.

Ready threshold: 8/10 and no hard fail. This score is QA, not a forecast.

## Signal rule
A visible workflow makes the offer relevant. A current event makes timing stronger. Seek a second trigger when possible: new branch, new service, campaign, hiring, expansion, migration, integration project or explicit request.

## Cold message architecture
**Trigger → Problem hypothesis → Buyer outcome → Mechanism → Proof → Interest CTA**

Do not sell abstractions such as “structured flow” when you can describe the buyer-visible result.

Example:
> На сайте запись идёт через WhatsApp. У нас есть рабочий flow, где клиент выбирает услугу и время до переписки, а администратор получает уже заполненный запрос. Могу отправить короткое демо?

## Persona map
- Owner: control, customer experience, operational friction, commercial result.
- Operations: incomplete requests, handoffs, statuses, repetitive coordination.
- Sales: qualification, follow-up, context, less back-and-forth.
- Technical buyer: API, ownership, deployment, security, integration risk.

## Proof kit
Every active offer should eventually have:
- one-line outcome,
- 30-60 sec proof,
- 3 representative screenshots,
- live demo link when available,
- one explicit limitation,
- one next-step CTA.

## CTA ladder
1. Cold: permission/interest.
2. Positive reply: send proof + one diagnostic question.
3. After demo: validate fit.
4. Qualified: specific next step/time.
5. Proposal: decision/commitment.

## Reply playbook
- Interested → send relevant proof, then one diagnostic question.
- Price? → answer with supported price/range or explain what must be scoped.
- We already have a system → ask about one gap/edge case; offer only a module/integration if relevant.
- Send information → send one concise asset, not a long proposal.
- Not interested → acknowledge and stop.
- Wrong person → ask who owns the workflow.
- Who are you? → one-line VIIVERSION identity + relevant proof.

## Follow-up cadence
Email default: day 0, 3, 7, 12, 19, 28.
Messenger default: day 0, 3, 8, 15.
Each touch must either bump briefly or add one new proof/angle. Do not resend the full pitch.

## Localization
Localize intent, hierarchy, honorifics and channel register, not just vocabulary. Vietnamese Zalo/Facebook copy must read naturally in Vietnamese and use appropriate anh/chị/bác sĩ addressing.

## CRO
Track:
- positive reply,
- demo accepted,
- qualified conversation,
- paid entry.

Reply rate is secondary; open rate is diagnostic. Test one meaningful variable at a time. Never call a message “10/10 commercially” before market data exists.
