# VIIVERSION Outreach Playbook v1

## Who owns what
- Product marketing: chooses ICP, problem, positioning and offer.
- Conversion copywriting: turns research into value proposition, message hierarchy and CTA.
- SDR/outbound: executes prospecting and follow-up.
- CRO: tests message/landing variants against conversion outcomes.
- Sales enablement: keeps templates, proof and rules consistent.

VIIVERSION combines these roles into one operating system. Copy is not written from taste; it is generated from evidence, rules and experiment results.

## Cold message architecture
Use: **Trigger → Problem hypothesis → Outcome/Mechanism → Proof → Interest CTA**.

The message is not a mini-proposal. Its job is to earn permission for the next useful step.

### Example
> На сайте запись идёт через WhatsApp. Мы уже собрали для похожего сервиса мобильный flow, где клиент выбирает услугу и время до начала переписки. Есть рабочее демо на телефоне. Отправить?

## CTA ladder
1. Cold: permission/interest.
2. Positive reply: send asset + one diagnostic question.
3. After demo: validate fit.
4. Qualified: ask for a specific next step/time.
5. Proposal: ask for a commercial decision or explicit next commitment.

## Offer formula
**For [buyer] with [observable situation], [offer] helps [outcome] by [mechanism], without [main friction]. Proof: [asset].**

Do not lead with AI, platform, CRM, automation, tech stack or a feature list unless the buyer is explicitly shopping for that category.

## Message quality gate
Score 0-10 from config/messaging.yaml. A cold message must score at least 8 and has hard-fail conditions.

## Follow-up
Email: up to ~6 touches over 14-28 days, each short; do not resend the same pitch.
Messenger: fewer touches; default max 4. Stop on explicit disinterest.

## Testing
Track positive replies, accepted demos, qualified conversations and paid entry. Open rate is diagnostic, not the commercial goal. Change one meaningful variable per A/B test.
